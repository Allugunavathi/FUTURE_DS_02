Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard 
📌 Project Overview

The Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze customer behavior, payment methods, tenure patterns, and churn trends.

This dashboard helps businesses understand why customers leave services and provides insights that support customer retention strategies and business decision-making.

🎯 Problem Statement

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by subscription-based and telecom businesses. High churn rates can reduce revenue and impact long-term growth.

The objective of this project was to transform raw customer data into an interactive dashboard that helps identify churn patterns, customer behavior trends, and factors influencing customer retention.

📈 Key Insights

The dataset contains 7,043 total customers.
The average monthly charges are approximately 64.76.
Customers have an average tenure of 32.37 months.
The average total charges are around 2.28K.
Customers using Electronic Check payment methods show higher churn rates compared to other payment methods.
The majority of customers are retained, while a smaller percentage represents churned customers.
Monthly charges tend to increase with customer tenure, indicating higher revenue contribution from long-term customers.
